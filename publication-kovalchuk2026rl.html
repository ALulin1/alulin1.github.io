Selected Topics in Reinforcement Learning: Practical Hands-on

Sergey V. Kovalchuk, Ashish T. S. Ireddy, Chao Li

Buk, 2025

Published textbook (PDF)

Publisher description

The textbook presents a practical hands-on to selected classes of problems in reinforcement learning with specific methods and algorithms extending basic curriculum in this area. Each topic covers a separate aspect of reinforcement learning application in the context of modeling the behavior of intelligent agents. The selected topics are considered as tasks to be studied in practical classes or as part of independent study.
