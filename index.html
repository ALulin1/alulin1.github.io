I am a Senior Researcher at Sirius University of Science and Technology and a Ph.D. Candidate at ITMO University. My research focuses on how norms emerge, spread, and guide decisions in multi-agent systems. I combine mathematical modelling, agent-based simulation, and machine learning to study collective behaviour and human–AI interaction, with applications in healthcare, software engineering and the social sciences. As a core member of the TacitAI project, I investigate decision modelling in distributed and hybrid cognitive systems under incomplete information.

Norm Dynamics in Multi-Agent Systems

Descriptive norms, norm perception and induction, and the connection between individual decisions and collective behaviour.
