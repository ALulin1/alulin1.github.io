Modelling the Dynamics of Software Developer Roles from Source-Code Repository Data

E. A. Tarasov, Chao Li, Sergey V. Kovalchuk

Modern Research and Technological Aspects of Software Engineering: Proceedings of the All-Russian Scientific and Technical Conference, pp. 338–343, Orenburg State University, 2026

Published paper (PDF)

English summary (prepared from the Russian text; not an original English abstract)

This Russian-language conference paper reviews approaches to identifying developer roles from source-code repository data and explores temporal collaboration norms in open-source communities. It analyses commit timing, work-hour and weekend activity, contributor roles, and geographical context, comparing patterns across GitHub repositories as a preliminary step toward modelling role and norm dynamics.
